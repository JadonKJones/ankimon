## 🌟 Ankimon v2.02-E 🌟

Big thanks to [@scotej](https://github.com/scotej) for this update! <3

### What's new

Pokémon now **evolve through friendship and time of day**, not just by level — raise a Pokémon's friendship as you study and it'll evolve at the right moment (Eevee → Espeon by day / Umbreon at night, plus Golbat, Pichu, Riolu, Munchlax and more). The **Pokémon PC** tells you *when and how* each one will evolve, highlights your highest-friendship **best friend**, lets you **sort by friendship**, shows a friendship bar in the details panel, and adds an **Evolve now** button so you can evolve on demand. There are also configurable **day/night and time-zone** settings, and this release folds in a batch of evolution / battle stability fixes.

> ℹ️ No data files changed — the friendship/time data already lived in `pokemon_evolution.csv`; this adds the code that reads and acts on it.
